Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information RH Land & Realty LLC ("RH Land & Realty," "we," "us") collects through rhlandandrealty.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

When you submit our "Get My Free Cash Offer" form, we collect the information you provide, which may include your name, phone number, email address, the county and state of your property, approximate acreage, and a description of your situation. We do not collect this information unless you choose to submit it.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to evaluate your property, prepare a cash offer, and contact you by phone, text message, or email about that offer and any resulting transaction. We do not use your information for any purpose unrelated to responding to your inquiry.

How it's handled

Form submissions are delivered to our inbox through a third-party form-processing service. We do not sell your information to third parties. If you move forward with a sale, we may share necessary details with service providers directly involved in closing the transaction, such as a title company.

Communications consent

By submitting the form, you agree that RH Land & Realty may contact you by phone, text message, or email regarding your submission. Message and data rates may apply. You can ask to stop receiving texts at any time by replying STOP, or by emailing us using the address below.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing rowan@rhlandrealtyteam.com.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults and are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to rowan@rhlandrealtyteam.com.
