About RH Land & Realty

Built for owners who just want the land handled.

RH Land & Realty is a land acquisition company for owners who inherited a parcel they never wanted or simply have acreage sitting unused. We buy vacant residential, commercial, and rural land directly — in any condition — and pay cash at closing.

Based in Connecticut, we've been buying land across the entire country since 2024. No listings, no showings, no waiting on a buyer's financing to fall through.
